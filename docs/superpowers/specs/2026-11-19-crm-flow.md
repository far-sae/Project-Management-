# CRM flow: lead → deal → project

Date: 2026-11-19. Branch: `redesign`.

## Why

The CRM existed but was hidden (no menu entry) and its parts did not connect: leads could not become deals, winning a deal did nothing, the hand-off made an empty project, the forecast was always zero, and a follow-up could not be saved until a delivery project existed. This is the flow other CRMs (HubSpot, Pipedrive) use, connected to project management at the end.

## The flow

```
Lead ──► Deal ──► Won ──► Project (delivery)
 │         │
 │         └─► Lost (with a reason)
 └─► Unqualified
```

1. **Lead** — a person you might do business with. Six steps, shown as one word:
   New → Contacted → Qualified → Deal open → Customer, or Unqualified.
   New / Contacted / Qualified / Unqualified are set by hand. "Deal open" and "Customer" happen by converting and winning.
2. **Convert to deal** — opens a deal in the pipeline linked to the lead and their company (creating the company if needed). The lead stays in the list as "Deal open".
3. **Deal** — moves through the pipeline stages. Each open stage has a chance of winning; the deal's chance follows its stage unless someone typed their own figure. Forecast = value × chance.
4. **Won** — moving a deal to a Won stage stamps the close date and opens the hand-off straight away. A won deal with no project is flagged "needs hand-off" on the board, the deal page, the company page and the CRM overview.
5. **Hand-off** — creates a project with the normal board columns, a brief (deal, value, customer, contact, notes) as the description and editable starter tasks, links the deal to it, and marks the lead a Customer. Linking to an existing project is the alternative. If the link step fails after the project was created, "Try again" reuses that project.
6. **Lost** — asks for a reason first. Reasons are counted on the CRM overview.
7. **Project → deal** — a project that came from a deal shows a "Deal: …" link in its header.

## Names

Leads (routes stay `/crm/contacts`), Companies, Deals, Follow-ups. Never "contract", "business" or "client" for these.

## Database

`supabase/migrations/180_crm_deal_close_fields.sql` adds `crm_deals.closed_at` and `crm_deals.lost_reason`. The app works without it (the write is repeated without those columns) but then does not keep the close date or the lost reason.

## Access

Sidebar entry "CRM" for owners and admins on plans with the `crm` feature. It no longer waits for the workspace to have a project. A workspace with a customised menu has to switch CRM on in its menu settings.

## Not done

- Per-member CRM access (members and viewers do not see it).
- Email or calendar sync, quotes, products, automation.
- Old-CRM leftovers that only reference each other: `src/components/deals/DealsListView.tsx`, `src/services/supabase/deals.ts`, `src/services/supabase/activities.ts` and their tests.
